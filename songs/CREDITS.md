- **Type**: Traditional Russian Folk Song

- **Public Domain**: ✅ Yes

- **Composer**: Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky

- **Composer**: Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky
- **Work**: "The Nutcracker" Ballet, Op. 71

- **Public Domain**: ✅ Yes
